Our Story

Fighting Food Waste Through Smart Connections.

Food doesn't belong in landfills. It belongs on someone's plate.

Every day, perfectly edible food is discarded while many people struggle to access nutritious meals. ReServe transforms this challenge into an opportunity by intelligently connecting food donors with NGOs, community kitchens, and nearby users.
